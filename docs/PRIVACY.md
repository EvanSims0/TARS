# TARS privacy policy

TARS is a personal voice assistant used by one person, its owner, on their own PC. It is not
offered to anyone else.

**What it accesses.** With the owner's permission, TARS reads and labels the owner's Gmail,
saves drafts and sends email after the owner confirms aloud, and reads and edits events on
the owner's Google Calendar.

**Where data goes.** Data stays on the owner's PC, except for what each request needs:
- request text goes to Anthropic (Claude) for answers
- speech audio goes to Deepgram for transcription
- reply text goes to ElevenLabs for speech

No data is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train models by TARS.

**What is stored.** Text transcripts are kept on the PC for 7 days. No raw audio is stored.
Remembered facts are kept in a local notes folder that the owner can edit or delete. Google
sign-in tokens are kept in the Windows credential store.

**Removing access.** The owner can revoke access at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the owner of this repository.
